Can a compact chest X-ray classifier transfer more reliably across hospitals while placing more of its evidence inside annotated lung-opacity regions?

Research question

Performance at one hospital is not enough.

The project tests whether higher native image resolution and source-only acquisition-style consistency training improve external pneumonia classification and make Grad-CAM evidence better aligned with public opacity annotations.

01

The clinical concern

Changes in population, acquisition and labeling can reduce performance outside the source dataset. A high score also does not reveal whether the model used the lesion or a shortcut.

Method

One architecture, controlled changes.

A one-channel ResNet18 is trained on PneumoniaMNIST. Resolution and training objectives are separated before frozen external evaluation.

Each source image is paired with two mild acquisition-style views. Cross-entropy learns the label; Jensen–Shannon consistency discourages predictions from following brightness, contrast, rotation, blur or small-noise changes.

Spatial evidence

Interpretability improves, but the result remains cautious.

AC-JSD improves average box-energy alignment and matched mean-fill occlusion over plain training. Aug1 has the strongest box alignment, while a simple center prior remains competitive.

The maps depend on learned parameters, but better heatmaps do not by themselves prove causal lesion recognition.
